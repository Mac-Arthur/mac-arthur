About

Engineering financial infrastructure that people can rely on

I'm a full-stack software engineer based in Accra, Ghana, specializing in secure, high-availability fintech platforms. My work centers on delivering cross-border payment integrations and agent-banking systems for banks and microfinance institutions across Africa — taking financial products from development all the way through production deployment.

I work primarily in C# and .NET on the backend, paired with modern JavaScript frameworks on the front end. Beyond writing code, I own delivery end to end: UI integration, configuration, testing, production migration, and post-deployment support — collaborating directly with regional banks and internal stakeholders to get real financial products live.

I also build outside of fintech — designing and shipping landing pages and websites for businesses and organizations that need a fast, polished web presence.
